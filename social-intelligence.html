The science behind it

Evidence for
automated prioritization.

Statistical risk control uses reviewed examples to establish how much triage your platform can automate within agreed error limits.

Two risks matter: priority mentions missed, and priority in the routine queue. Each uses a different denominator.

The supported policy is tied to a defined model, priority rules and population, with a process for reassessment when conditions change.

Evidence, scope and adoption effort

Existing reviewed examples may be reusable after checking label quality, model version, sampling, languages and sources. New customer populations or priority definitions may need new evidence. The amount of review and integration work depends on the workflow and error limits.

We would agree the evaluation baseline, data access and responsibilities before integration. Incident response stays with the organization responsible for acting on the mention; the provider, customer and any service team agree who supplies labels and carries out continuing reviews and audits.
