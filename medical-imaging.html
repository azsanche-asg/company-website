What exactly would the guarantee cover?

Each task needs its own error definition, reference protocol, population, model/scorer versions and statistical assumptions. OCT and fundus concern errors in a selected group before audits. Chest X-ray concerns reference-label inclusion across all in-scope studies.

Account for patient and site dependence, acquisition devices, image quality, related scans and prior use of the data. Calibration and an independent assessment must support the intended claim; real research images alone do not establish it.

These guarantees do not establish correct diagnoses for every patient, complete an examination or replace clinical and deployment approvals. A model or population change can require reassessment.

What you bring

One workflow. Its evidence.

  • The modality, finding or grade, intended population and the decision you want to change.
  • Original images or governed source access, versioned AI outputs and compatible scores.
  • Independent reference reviews, disagreements, quality flags and patient/site groupings.
  • Current checks, unresolved outputs, review volumes and clinical/product owners.

What the evaluation delivers

A basis for a deployment decision.

  • The supported task-specific risk statement and scope, where evidence permits it.
  • A comparison of your existing process, score-only policy and calibrated policy.
  • Errors, unresolved outputs, checks, audits, corrections and clinical time.
  • Integration and monitoring requirements, with a recommendation on a monitored pilot or further preparation.

No fixed saving, label count or deployment time is assumed. The result may support reduced checking, require further evidence or retain the current process.
