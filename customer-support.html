What does adoption involve?

Before sending: access to the complete AI draft, conversation and current source records. Agree how the reply enters review or reaches the customer.

During operation: your team handles customers, exceptions and audits. Changes to the model, source policies or request mix trigger reconsideration and, when needed, reassessment.

Evidence: use independently reviewed examples of routine replies as well as errors. Scope languages and channels explicitly; assess reuse before applying earlier evidence to a changed workflow.
