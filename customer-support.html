What would a real statistical guarantee mean?

A justified assessment would bound the rate of the defined material errors in the policy-selected population, under explicit sampling and selection assumptions. It would not certify every reply, all support quality or every language separately.

For a valid 95% confidence procedure, the bound covers the defined population risk in at least 95% of repeated evidence samples under its assumptions. This does not mean that each reply is 95% correct or that future population changes are covered.

Choosing a 1% limit and observing less than 1% in one sample do not establish that guarantee. The bound, assessment method and selection procedure must also support it.

Do fewer reviews actually save time?

Measure the task that disappears. A reply check is not a complete support interaction. If an agent still needs to investigate or resolve the ticket, that work remains.

Recurring work

Retained reply reviews, audits, corrections, adjudication, monitoring and investigations. Difficult exceptions may take longer than the routine checks removed.

Setup and reassessment

Pairing replies with original source snapshots, labelling, scorer evaluation, integration and further evidence rounds. Record these separately and state the period over which setup is recovered.

Scoring, hosting and integration charges belong in the cost comparison. Added scoring latency needs its own measurement. No handling-time reduction, staffing saving, CSAT improvement or payback period is measured here.

Where does the risk score come from?

In this demo, every score is authored. There is no live AI judge, scorer training or model inference. The original reference labels do not generate a score; they are separate demonstration data.

  1. Original AI replyPaired order state and applicable policy at the time of the draft
  2. Frozen scorerRanks the risk of a material incorrect or unsupported claim
  3. Reviewed evidenceAssesses whether a selected rule can support the requested risk limit

A real scorer could use model signals, evidence checks or a separate evaluator. An existing scorer could be assessed, or a suitable one would need preparation. This walkthrough does not establish that a production support scorer or connector is available.

A score of 0.01 is not a demonstrated 1% error probability. A ranking score alone does not provide a statistical guarantee. Its ability to separate lower-risk replies must be tested against independently reviewed references, including subtle omissions and conflicting sources.

The scorer and reply generator must be versioned and held fixed for the assessment. Evaluation independence comes from the data and review process; merely using a different AI model as judge does not establish it. If the scorer cannot separate errors well enough, the requested limit may support little or no automation.

What would connect to our support system?
  1. Your reply AIProduces a complete, unsent draft with its conversation and source snapshots.
  2. Risk assessmentUses the frozen scorer and a compatible assessed policy. Records the original reply and decision.
  3. Your helpdeskSends the exact permitted draft, or assigns human handling. Keeps customer-service responsibilities.

The integration needs

Stable conversation, customer, order and attempt identifiers; original draft and content version; timestamped source/policy snapshots; model, prompt, scorer and mapping versions; language and workflow scope. Bind the decision to that exact content and context, and make retries idempotent.

A changed draft or stale decision needs a new assessment. Missing evidence, scorer timeout or an unavailable review queue never permits an automatic send. When the queue fails, keep the draft pending and alert the responsible team.

What must be measured

Scoring and network latency, timeout behaviour, peak-load capacity, review backlog and recovery. Choose timeouts and service targets with the customer; this demo measures none of them.

Relay Automations and Relay Desk are fictional stand-ins for your workflow and helpdesk. The browser implements local routing, review and receipt behaviour only. Production connectors, identity controls and incident response need an agreed integration.

Complete text replies first

The example covers English web chat. Streaming replies and voice need a decision before any relevant content reaches the customer, plus a suitable timing and evidence design. Support for those channels is not established here.

Can existing evidence cover new topics, languages or versions?

Reuse is assessed, not automatic. Previous records can help develop the scorer or understand failures if their use is permitted. Before using them as evidence for a new claim, check the error definition, source snapshots, model and scorer versions, population, label quality, dependence and prior use in selection.

A new language, market, policy or reply category may need new references and a separate assessment. A pooled guarantee does not establish a guarantee for every segment. Segment-specific suspension would need separately identified scopes, assessments and routing; this demo pauses the whole Northline deployment.

Plan reviewer and adjudicator time, routine sampling and unresolved cases. The required evidence depends on the target risk, scorer, error frequency and sampling design. The demo’s 3,000 authored records are not a label-count or deployment-time commitment.

Evidence and incident history remain inspectable after a change. They do not automatically qualify the new workflow for operation.

What continues after activation?

Reviews and audits remain. Your team handles exceptions, pre-send audits, disputed labels and customer follow-up. It watches review capacity and response times, especially during demand spikes.

Known version or input changes can invalidate a policy immediately when detected. A change in customer requests can emerge later through reviewed errors, complaints or monitored scope changes. Detection can be delayed; some replies may already have been delivered.

Pause the affected deployment, preserve the evidence and investigate. Reviewers need the original draft and its original source snapshots, plus clearly dated new evidence. Fresh context can change the response a customer needs without rewriting the original assessment.

Suspension stops new automatic sends; it does not retract messages. Your service team owns any customer correction or follow-up. Recovery requires a compatible evidence plan, a new assessment, route tests and explicit activation. It can require more evidence or fail to support the requested automation.
