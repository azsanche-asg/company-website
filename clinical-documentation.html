The additional capability

Your scribe creates the note.
We establish the supported checking policy.

Quality scores, source links and clinician review already help teams find errors. EyeTrustAI uses reviewed source-and-draft evidence to establish a statistical limit for an agreed class of errors in drafts eligible to skip an additional structured check.

Compare your current rules, the scorer alone and the calibrated policy on the same independent encounters. Establish both the supported error risk and the work that would actually disappear.

Define the error

Which facts must the draft preserve?

Start with medication, dose or allergy discrepancies against the supplied source. Agree what counts as an error and how disagreements are resolved.

Establish the limit

Which drafts need an extra check?

Assess a versioned scorer and calibrate the checking policy for an agreed population. If the evidence cannot support reduced checking, retain it.

Measure the value

What improves over today’s process?

Count additional checks, audits, correction work and clinician time. The comparison starts from your actual controls, including any existing automation.

What exactly would the guarantee cover?

The target is the proportion of original drafts with an agreed medication or allergy error among all drafts eligible to skip the additional source check, before audit selection. A later audit or correction does not erase the original error.

Agree the source, note type, language, specialty, error definition, model/template/scorer versions and statistical statement. Calibration needs suitable reference reviews and assumptions about how the evidence represents future work; repeated patients and related encounters must be accounted for.

This is source fidelity. A faithful note can still reflect an incomplete or incorrect source. The guarantee does not establish treatment correctness, error-free individual notes or permission to remove clinician sign-off.

What you bring

What the evaluation delivers

A basis for a deployment decision.

  • The supported risk bound and scope, where the evidence permits one.
  • An independent comparison with current controls and the scorer alone.
  • Additional checks, audits, corrections, clinician time and operating costs.
  • Integration, monitoring, fallback and ownership requirements; a recommendation on the next step.

No fixed saving, label count or deployment time is assumed. The result may support reduced checking, require further evidence or retain the current process.
