About EyeTrustAI

AI systems need a clearer
account of uncertainty.

We are building the statistical foundations and product experiences that help teams understand what their AI can support—and the evidence behind it.

Our perspective

Make uncertainty
part of the system.

A prediction becomes more useful when people can understand its uncertainty, evaluate the relevant risk, and inspect the assumptions behind a statistical claim.

EyeTrustAI brings together conformal prediction, conformal risk control, and the practical work of preparing evidence, evaluating outputs, and maintaining their context.

We explore that foundation through tools for AI builders and focused applications for teams working with AI in consequential settings.

How we work

Precision in the claim.
Clarity in the experience.

01 / Scientific care

State the assumptions.

02 / Practical usefulness

Show the trade-off.

Measure the size, width, workload, and evidence requirements that make a statistical result useful in practice.

03 / Inspectable evidence

Make the work reproducible.

Publish research artifacts that allow others to examine calibration, reproduce aggregate results, and understand limitations.
