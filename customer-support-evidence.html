Reviews are counted as routing tasks. This experiment includes no audits, time estimates or assumptions about reviewer accuracy. Production costs and retained work need their own comparison.

Method

What was calculated, and from what.

Public support enquiries

BANKING77 contains English banking queries with 77 intent labels. After exact-text deduplication: 6,999 training examples, 3,000 calibration examples and 3,072 held-out test examples.

A fixed, transparent model

Word TF-IDF and logistic regression. Maximum class probability ranks predictions; it is not treated as a calibrated probability of correctness.

Selection-aware bounds

Exact one-sided binomial upper bounds for ten fixed score thresholds. A Bonferroni correction gives 95% simultaneous confidence under the stated assumptions.

Sampling, validity and scope

The error is a predicted intent that differs from the dataset label. The target is the error rate conditional on selection. We choose the threshold retaining the most calibration examples among those whose upper bound meets the target.

For k errors among n selected calibration examples, the bound is the 1 − 0.05/10 quantile of Beta(k + 1, n − k). If n = 0 or k = n, it is 1. The ten bounds hold simultaneously with at least 95% confidence under independent sampling, so selection among them is accounted for.

The population interpretation assumes independently sampled queries from the same distribution, correct reference labels, and a model and threshold family fixed before calibration. The public dataset does not establish those conditions for any customer. Conversation-level identifiers are unavailable; near-duplicates and correlated requests may remain.

The official test set has a balanced intent design. Its aggregate rates describe this benchmark, not the class mix of a deployment. There is no guarantee for each intent, language, future request mix or individual prediction.

Four duplicate training texts, seven test texts overlapping training/calibration, and one duplicate test text were removed using a predeclared normalization rule. No model or threshold settings were tuned after seeing calibration or test results.

Reproduce it

The calculation is inspectable.

The code downloads a pinned dataset version, writes the protocol, trains the classifier, selects policies and then evaluates the test set. The result files include every candidate bound.

The next step · Your own evidence

Establish what your replies
can support at your risk limit.

Start with one reply type, language and delivery channel. Keep your existing controls in the comparison.

  1. Agree the error and population.

    Define material wrong or unsupported claims, trusted source records and the exact group of original replies covered by the risk target.

  2. Prepare independent evidence.

    Pair drafts with contemporaneous facts and policies. Review routine cases as well as errors, keep related conversations together and separate development, calibration and evaluation.

  3. Calculate and compare.

    Freeze the scorer and candidate policies before calibration. Use a method suited to the selected population, then inspect held-out errors, automation and review work against current controls.

  4. Decide what is useful.

    Include audits, integration, latency and operating costs. A supported bound and a worthwhile workflow are both needed before moving to a monitored pilot.
