What a statistical claim means

Precise by design.

Every method establishes a particular property under particular assumptions. We make that scope part of the product experience.

Inspect the research ↗
Does a coverage target apply to every prediction?

Marginal coverage concerns the population of examples under the method’s assumptions. It does not give a separate probability of correctness for each individual example, class, or subgroup.

How does conformal risk control differ?

Conformal risk control can target expected bounded losses for suitable monotone output families. The exact loss and statistical statement matter. Expected loss, marginal coverage, and error conditional on selected outputs are distinct quantities.

What happens when the model or data change?

The calibration evidence has a scope. A version or population change may require a new assessment and calibration. The demonstrations show version binding and new evidence rounds; they do not establish universal guarantees under distribution shift.

What can I use today?

You can explore the interactive demonstrations and reproduce the public research artifacts. The broader platform is under development. Contact us to discuss the fit and scope of a pilot for your system.
