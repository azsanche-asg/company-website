The guarantee has a defined scope

Choose the right
statistical statement.

Coverage, average omission and error among selected outputs answer different questions. The method, population and reference define what a result means.

See the validation studies
What does a statistical guarantee cover?

A defined risk or coverage property for a specified population, under the method’s assumptions. Coverage concerns whether the reference falls inside a set or interval. Expected-loss control concerns an average loss, such as omitted anatomy. Error among selected outputs requires a method that controls that particular subset. None means every individual output is correct.

What do the walkthroughs calculate?

ForgeSight and Voltara calculate split-conformal sets and intervals. ContourWorks calculates a region using conformal risk control. Each uses a disclosed synthetic dataset and shows separate evaluation results, including misses.

What changes when the model changes?

The output artifact is tied to a model version, evidence and method. The walkthroughs show version checks and new evidence rounds. They do not establish guarantees under arbitrary distribution shift.

What is available today?

Complete interactive demonstrations and public research artifacts. The shared platform is in development. Our focused solutions serve teams operating AI in their products and workflows.
