Evidence & evaluation

Two risks, two different questions.

The HumAID benchmark evaluates priority mentions among those removed from immediate review. The walkthrough explores that risk alongside the share of all priority mentions missed.

Priority in the routine queue

Priority mentions not sent for immediate review ÷ all mentions in that routine queue.

Missed priority mentions

Priority mentions not sent for immediate review ÷ all priority mentions.

A calculated result on HumAID

At a 5% target, 1,327 of 9,559 test mentions were selected for reduced review. Of those, 45 were priority mentions: 3.39%. The calibration upper bound was 3.85% at 95% confidence.

Observed priority retention was 99.28%. This experiment did not certify that second quantity or establish savings in a commercial listening product.

Reading the walkthrough

The workflow uses prepared policy outcomes and example mentions. Its workload counts illustrate reviews, audits and missed priority mentions together; they are not customer measurements.

A joint guarantee requires support for both risks with an agreed joint confidence level. Correlated posts, reposts and repeated incidents must be reflected in the sampling and statistical procedure.

A fair comparison

Compare existing priority rules, the score alone and the risk-controlled policy on the same held-out population. Include analyst triage, audits, investigation and retained priority mentions. Record setup and reassessment separately.
