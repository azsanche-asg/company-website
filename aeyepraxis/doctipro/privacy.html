AEyePraxis Doctipro connection

Privacy notice for the Chrome extension · 8 November 2026

This extension connects the practice’s Doctipro session to its AEyePraxis workspace. Its publisher is the individual developer identified on the Chrome Web Store listing. EyeTrustAI is the product brand.

What the extension accesses

When a user requests an import, the extension reads patient identity and contact fields, including the Luxembourg Matricule, and the selected clinical documents from the approved Doctipro site. For a selected fiche médicale, the extension reads its full printed PDF, including embedded images, and the clinical fields of that fiche. It sends these directly to the practice’s AEyePraxis tab. At the user’s request, it can fill a note or prescription draft in the Doctipro session. The practice reviews the result there.

To connect, the extension checks the approved site addresses and available tabs. It uses the user’s existing Doctipro session. It does not request a Doctipro password or read browser cookies.

Optional calculator transfers

If the user approves calculator access and selects a calculator, the extension sends the reviewed eye, lens and biometry measurements to the selected ESCRS, ASCRS or IOLCon website. The calculator transfer does not include a name or Matricule.

Storage and sharing

Chrome stores the approved site addresses locally and uses temporary session storage for transfers. Imported records are stored by the practice’s AEyePraxis software. The extension has no analytics or advertising service. It does not sell patient data or send it to the publisher. Remove the extension in Chrome to remove its local connection settings. The practice manages records it has already imported.

Contact the publisher about the extension through the EyeTrustAI contact page. Please omit patient information from support requests.
