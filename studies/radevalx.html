What we tested

The corrected binary SCoRE-SDR method was compared with classical marginal CRC on the same previously used 30 calibration and 30 locked radiology reports. The RadCliQ score is reference-assisted. Selections were frozen before the evaluation labels were joined.

What the evidence supports

Classical CRC targeted harmful accepts per processed report. Binary SCoRE-SDR targeted expected selective deployment risk. Both small-sample constraints and the score’s ordering prevented nonzero selection by the selective method. Zero selected-set loss uses the theorem’s convention; conditional error is undefined when nothing is selected.

The statistical statement, in more detail

This is an expectation guarantee under the declared method-specific benchmark assumptions. The two methods do not certify the same quantity and their percentages are not interchangeable.

Observed evaluation error, calibration support and practical output size measure different things. The study’s declared population and reference determine how its result should be read.

Scope of the result

A reference-assisted score is not a demonstrated reference-free runtime scorer. Cohort reuse makes this a retrospective method comparison. No real-time, individual-report, clinical or operational benefit is established.
