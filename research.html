What does a stricter error limit cost in automation?

Evidence & time

NEISS

More evidence makes calibration more stable.

4.93% set misses; a single label on 50.50% of 10,000 held-out records.

NTSB

What happens when a calibrated policy gets older?

6.43%, 4.63% and 5.40% observed set misses for three calibration vintages.
